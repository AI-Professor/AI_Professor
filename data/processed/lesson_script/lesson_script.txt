"Let's imagine a scene where a man is in agony, pleading with a spectral figure. This man is Scrooge, a gruff and cold-hearted individual. Suddenly, he finds himself in a desperate conflict, grappling with this ghostly figure and begging for his fate to be altered. Miraculously, the phantom shrinks and turns into a bedpost, revealing that Scrooge is back in his own room. He feels a wave of relief and happiness wash over him. The experience transformed him; he promises to live in the past, present, and future with the spirit of Christmas in his heart. From this, we learn the lesson that it's never too late to change and we should always strive to be better versions of ourselves."
