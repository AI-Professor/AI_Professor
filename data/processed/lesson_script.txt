"Let's imagine ourselves in a room, our protagonist, Scrooge, is gripping a spectral hand, pleading for his fate to be reversed. He's a man tormented by his past, present, and future, a man who's been visited by spirits sent through Jacob Marley’s intervention. The conflict arises as Scrooge battles with these spirits, his fear, and his regret, desperately trying to change his destiny. The turning point comes as the phantom that Scrooge is struggling with transforms into his own bedpost. He wakes up, finding himself in his own bed, in his own room, with time at his disposal to make amends. The story teaches us that it's never too late to change our ways and make amends, as Scrooge promises to live in the past, present, and future, and let the spirits of all three strive within him."
